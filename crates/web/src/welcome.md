# Welcome to notekpr 👋

This is **My notes**, your first vault: a place for notes, drawings and files.
Everything you put here is **encrypted on this device** before it's synced.
The server keeps it, but can't read it: not your notes, not their names, not
even this vault's name.

Tick things off as you try them, and delete this note whenever you like.

## Write

- [ ] Just start typing. The toolbar formats text, and Markdown works too: `**bold**`, `# Heading`, `- list`
- [ ] Make a new note with **New note** in the sidebar (`Ctrl+Alt+N`)
- [ ] Link notes: type `[[` and a name, like [[My first idea]]. A link to a note that doesn't exist yet makes it when you click it
- [ ] Add #tags anywhere, and find them again with `Ctrl+Shift+F`: `tag:tags`
- [ ] Type `@today` for today's date
- [ ] Drop or paste a picture, a recording or any file into a note

## Capture and draw

- [ ] **Quick notes** (`Alt+Q`): jot something down from anywhere. They're yours alone, in no vault, until you move one into a vault
- [ ] **Drawings**: the drawing button next to *New note* starts one. `![[Its name.drawing]]` shows it in a note, and *Drawings* in the sidebar shows them all
- [ ] Drawings are compatible with Excalidraw: copy and paste between the two, import Excalidraw files, and export a drawing for Excalidraw (the ⤓ at the top of a drawing)

## Find your way

- [ ] Jump to any note with `Ctrl+P`; `Ctrl+/` lists every shortcut
- [ ] `Ctrl+E` switches between editing and reading
- [ ] `Ctrl+G` shows how your notes link, as a graph
- [ ] Star a note (☆ at the top) to keep it under *Favorites*
- [ ] Every note keeps its versions: the clock at the top compares and restores them
- [ ] Deleted notes wait in the trash until you empty it

## Work together

- [ ] Share this vault: *Share vault* at the bottom of the sidebar. Choose who can edit and who can only read
- [ ] Or share a single note: the share button at the top of the note
- [ ] Write at the same time: you see each other's cursors, and edits merge, even after working offline
- [ ] Comment on some text: select it and press `Alt+M`. Type `@` and a name to mention someone

## Your devices, your files

- [ ] Keep your **recovery key** safe: it's the only way back in if you lose every device
- [ ] Sign in on another device: approve it from this one, or unlock it with your recovery key
- [ ] *Settings → Devices*: keep this browser usable offline
- [ ] Prefer your own editor? The `notekpr` app for your computer syncs your notes with a folder of plain Markdown files (Obsidian, VS Code, …)
- [ ] *Settings → AI agents*: give an assistant access to only the vaults you choose
- [ ] Take everything with you: *Settings → Account → Data* exports your vaults as Markdown (`.zip`)

On a Mac, press `⌘` where it says `Ctrl`.
